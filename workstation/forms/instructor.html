适用课程与授权范围 PROGRAMS & AUTHORIZED SCOPE

ACE、SNC、循证力量训练专家、50+训练专家、女性训练专家等专业课程的助教工作；以及经教培中心批准的配套线上直播课程、动作实操课程和专题工作坊的独立授课任务。

Assistant teaching in ACE, SNC, Evidence-Based Strength Training Specialist, 50+ Training Specialist and Women’s Training Specialist courses; plus independently delivered, approved online live sessions, practical training classes and specialist workshops.

见习期间主要职责 KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
01
主体课程教学支持SUPPORT IN CORE COURSES

协助主讲讲师开展课堂指导与实操教学；负责动作示范、动作技术部分的独立教学，并指导学员练习、提供纠错与反馈。

Support lead lecturers in classroom instruction and practical training; independently teach assigned movement-technique segments, demonstrate exercises, coach practice and provide corrective feedback.

02
经授权的独立教学AUTHORIZED INDEPENDENT TEACHING

在批准的课程、教学单元及工作坊范围内，独立承担动作实操课、专题工作坊及相关线上直播课程的授课、练习组织与学习效果检查。

Independently deliver approved practical classes, specialist workshops and related live online sessions; organize practice and assess learning within the assigned scope.

03
教学准备与岗位责任PREPARATION & ACCOUNTABILITY

完成课前准备、教学安全检查、课后复盘及学员学习反馈；按要求提交教学记录、每周工作汇报，接受导师指导和阶段评价。

Prepare sessions, check instructional safety, review outcomes and learner feedback; submit teaching records and weekly reports, and participate in mentoring and evaluation.
